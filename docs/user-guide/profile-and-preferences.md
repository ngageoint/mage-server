# Profile & Preferences

[← Back to Navigating MAGE](../Navigating_MAGE.md)

## What's Profile & Preferences?

**Profile** holds your personal account details — display name, email, phone number, and password. **Preferences** controls how the app behaves for you — update frequency, time format, time zone, and coordinate display. Both are reached from the account toolbar at the top right of the MAGE home page.

**Note:** your username is shown on your Profile but can't be changed there — it's read-only.

## Updating Your Profile

1. Open the account toolbar and choose **Profile**.
2. Update your **Display Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, or avatar photo as needed.
3. Click **Save** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them.

**Changing your password:** if your account uses local sign-in (not single sign-on), a separate **Password** section is available. Enter your current password, then your new password twice, and click **Reset Password**. Changing your password signs you out immediately — you'll need to log back in with the new one.

## Setting Preferences

1. Open the account toolbar and choose **Preferences** — it slides in from the right.
2. Each setting is a dropdown that applies as soon as you pick a value, with no separate Save step:
   - **Update Frequency** — how often MAGE polls for updates: 5 Seconds, 30 Seconds, 2 Minutes, or 5 Minutes.
   - **Time Format** — Absolute or Relative timestamps.
   - **Time Zone** — Local or GMT.
   - **Coordinate System** — Lat/Lng, MGRS, or DMS.

## Common Issues

**Changing my password signed me out.** This is expected — MAGE signs you out as soon as a password change succeeds, and you'll need to log back in with your new password.

**I don't see a Password section in my Profile.** This section only appears for accounts using local (username/password) sign-in. If your account uses single sign-on or another external auth method, password changes are handled by that provider instead.
